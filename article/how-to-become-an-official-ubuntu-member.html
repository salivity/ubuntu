How to Become an Official Ubuntu Member

Becoming an official Ubuntu Member is a recognized way to acknowledge significant and sustained contributions to the Ubuntu community and ecosystem. This guide outlines the complete process of applying for Ubuntu Membership, from building a contribution history and creating your documentation to gathering testimonials and presenting your application before a Membership Board.

1. Demonstrate Sustained Contribution

Before applying, you must demonstrate a sustained and significant contribution to the Ubuntu project over a meaningful period of time (typically at least 6 months). Contributions are not limited to code and can include:

  • Software development and package maintenance
  • Bug reporting, triaging, and testing
  • Community support on forums, Ask Ubuntu, or IRC/Matrix
  • Technical writing, documentation, and translations
  • Event organization, local community (LoCo) leadership, and advocacy
  • Graphic design, artwork, and user experience work

2. Sign the Code of Conduct and Set Up Profiles

You must set up your identity within the ecosystem to track your work:

  • Launchpad Account: Create an active Launchpad account and sign the Ubuntu Code of Conduct using a GPG key.
  • Ubuntu Wiki Page: Create a personal page on the official Ubuntu Wiki. This page acts as your application portfolio and must include your personal background, a clear list of your contributions with supporting links, and your future plans within the community.

3. Gather Testimonials

Reach out to current Ubuntu Members, project leads, or community members who have worked with you. Ask them to write endorsements detailing your work, character, and collaboration skills directly on your Ubuntu Wiki page. Testimonials provide the board with firsthand confirmation of your impact.

4. Add Yourself to a Board Agenda

Select the appropriate review board based on your region or area of contribution:

  • Regional Membership Boards: Americas, EMEA (Europe, Middle East, and Africa), or APAC (Asia-Pacific).
  • Developer Membership Board: For applicants focusing on package maintenance, upload rights, and core development.

Find an upcoming meeting date on the chosen board's wiki page and add your name, Launchpad profile link, and Wiki application link to the agenda.

5. Attend the Review Meeting

Join the designated IRC or Matrix channel at the scheduled meeting time. The process proceeds as follows:

  • The board chair will introduce your application.
  • You will provide a brief summary of your work.
  • Board members will review your Wiki, ask questions about your contributions, and open the floor for short discussion.
  • The board will cast a vote during the meeting.

6. Next Steps After the Vote

  • If Approved: Your membership will be registered. You gain access to an @ubuntu.com email alias, voting rights in community elections, syndication on Planet Ubuntu, and official recognition within the community.
  • If Deferred: The board will offer constructive feedback explaining what additional experience or documentation is needed. You may reapply once you have addressed their suggestions.
